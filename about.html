Our Journey

From Humble Beginnings to Industry Leader

Founded in 1976 in the Kingdom of Bahrain, Al Nisr began as a specialist rolling shutter manufacturer serving local contractors. Through decades of commitment to quality, we grew to become Bahrain's most trusted name in aluminium fabrication and metal supply.

Today, our portfolio spans industrial warehouses, premium retail outlets, private villas on Amwaj Islands, and everything in between — each project executed with precision and care.

1976
Company Founded

Al Nisr Aluminium established in Bahrain, beginning with rolling shutter fabrication.

Product Expansion

Expanded into sectional overhead doors, compact doors, and window systems.
